## EXAM

Nasogastric Tube Placement

## COMPARISON

None.

## FINDINGS

Viscous lidocaine 2% gel was applied to the right naris and Cetacaine sprayed into the mouth. 
 A 10 French nasogastric tube was advanced under fluoroscopic guidance into the stomach. The sidehole was seen below the level of the left hemidiaphragm. Placement was further confirmed to be within the stomach by infusion of a small amount of water soluble contrast. The NG tube was flushed with water and secured using an adhesive bandage. 
 Fluoroscopy time:  minutes

## IMPRESSION

Nasogastric tube placement. Ready for immediate use.
