## EXAM

AAST Kidney Injury Grade

## COMPARISON

None.

## FINDINGS

No kidney injury 
 Contusion (microscopic or gross hematuria, urologic studies normal), or Subcapsular hematoma (nonexpanding without parenchymal laceration) 
 Hematoma (nonexpanding perirenal hematoma confined to renal retroperitoneum), Laceration (<1.0 cm parenchymal depth of renal cortex without urinary extravasation), or Multiple 'Grade 1' injuries. 
 Laceration (<1.0 cm parenchymal depth of renal cortex without collecting system rupture or urinary extravasation), or Multiple 'Grade 2' injuries. 
 Parenchymal laceration (extending through renal cortex, medulla, and collecting system), or Main renal artery or vein injury with contained hemorrhage. 
 Completely shattered kidney, or Avulsion of renal hilum which devascularizes kidney.
