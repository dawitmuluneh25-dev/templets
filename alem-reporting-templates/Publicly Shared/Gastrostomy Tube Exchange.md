## EXAM

Gastrostomy Tube Exchange

## COMPARISON

None.

## FINDINGS

The patient was placed supine on the fluoroscopy table. The balloon of the  French gastrostomy tube was deflated and the tube was removed. 
 A new  French gastrostomy tube was inserted to the percutaneous tract. The new gastrostomy tube balloon was inflated with dilute contrast material and pulled firmly up against the anterior abdominal wall. Contrast was then injected through the tube to confirm tube placement within the stomach. The external rubber bumper was then adjusted to approximately 3 mm from the skin surface and secured with a cable tie. The tube was flushed with water. 
 Fluoroscopy time:  minutes

## IMPRESSION

Fluoroscopically-guided exchange of percutaneous gastrostomy tube. The tube is ready for immediate use.
