## EXAM

Endoscopic-Retrograde-Cholangiogram-(ERC)

## TECHNIQUE

Images are submitted for review from endoscopic retrograde cholangiogram (ERC) performed by the GI lab. Endoscope is passed in an antegrade fashion, and the tip of the endoscope lies in the second portion of the duodenum. The distal bile duct is cannulated, and contrast injected in retrograde fashion.
