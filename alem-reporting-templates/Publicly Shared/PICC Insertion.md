## EXAM

PICC Insertion

## TECHNIQUE

Site:  Right arm Basilic vein  
 Catheter: single-lumen 
 PICC placement 
 Peripherally placed PICC line. The arm was prepped and draped in sterile fashion. Lidocaine 1% was used for local anesthetic. Under fluoroscopic and ultrasound guidance, the vein was patent and accessed with a micropuncture needle. A guide wire was then advanced into the vein. A vascular sheath was then advanced over a guide wire, and a PICC line was trimmed. The PICC line was then advanced into the central venous system. After confirmation of the catheter position, the catheter was sutured in place at the skin entry site.

## COMPARISON

None.

## FINDINGS

Position: A final placement radiograph demonstrates the tip of the catheter at the junction of SVC and right atrium. 
 Complications: No immediate complication.  
 Fluoroscopy time:  min
