## EXAM

Parathyroid SPECT

## CLINICAL INFORMATION

Hyperparathyroidism. Evaluate for parathyroid adenoma.

## COMPARISON

None.

## FINDINGS

Thyroid tracer washout: Delayed images demonstrate near-complete tracer washout from the thyroid. 
 Parathyroid uptake: None. The two-hour delayed images do not demonstrate any focal abnormal persistent uptake in the region of the parathyroid glands to suggest parathyroid adenoma. 
 Normal uptake: There is physiological tracer uptake in the myocardium, liver, salivary glands, and thyroid gland.

## IMPRESSION

Normal parathyroid imaging study. No evidence for mediastinal uptake to suggest mediastinal parathyroid adenoma.
