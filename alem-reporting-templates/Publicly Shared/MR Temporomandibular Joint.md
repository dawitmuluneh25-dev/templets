## EXAM

MR Temporomandibular Joint

## CLINICAL INFORMATION

Arthralgia. Joint popping.

## COMPARISON

None.

## FINDINGS

Right temporomandibular joint:The articular disc is normally positioned between the mandibular condyle and the temporal bone in both open and closed positions. There is normal anterior translation of the mandibular condyle with jaw opening. 
 Left temporomandibular joint: The articular disc is normally positioned between the mandibular condyle and the temporal bone in both open and closed positions. There is normal anterior translation of the mandibular condyle with jaw opening.

## IMPRESSION

Normal temporomandibular joints.
