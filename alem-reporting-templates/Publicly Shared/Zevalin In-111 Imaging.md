## EXAM

Zevalin In-111 Imaging

## TECHNIQUE

Dose 5 mCi

## COMPARISON

None.

## FINDINGS

Early images 
 The early images obtained at two hours postinjection show normal distribution of indium-111 Zevalin in the blood pool, liver, spleen, and kidneys. 
 Delayed images 
 Delayed images obtained at 48 hours after injection show normal uptake in the liver, spleen, and bladder.

## IMPRESSION

Normal tracer activity in the blood pool, liver, spleen and bladder is identified.
