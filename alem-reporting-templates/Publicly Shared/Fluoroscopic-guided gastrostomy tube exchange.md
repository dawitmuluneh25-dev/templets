## EXAM

Fluoroscopic-guided gastrostomy tube exchange

## TECHNIQUE

Using image guidance, the existing gastrotomy tube was exchanged for new gastrotomy tube.  A small amount of water soluable contrast was used to confirm appropriate placement within the stomach.

## COMPARISON

None.

## FINDINGS

Pre-procedure Information:  
 Informed consent : The risks, benefits, and alternatives of the procedure including procedural sedation were discussed with the patient. Verbal and written consent was obtained. 
 Time-out: Time-out performed to confirm the correct patient, procedure, and site.

## IMPRESSION

Fluoroscopic-guided percutaneous gastrostomy tube exchange. The new gastrostomy tube is ready for immediate use.
