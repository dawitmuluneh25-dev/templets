Definition of terms:
Normal: Bone mineral density that is within 1 standard deviation of young normals. (T-score greater than -1.0). The probability of fracture is considered to be equal to that of young normal individuals.
 
Osteopenia: Bone mineral density, which is between 1 and 2.5 standard deviations below young normal control patients (T-score between -1 and -2.5). The possibility of fracture is 2 to 4 times that of young normal patients.
 
Osteoporosis: Bone mineral density that is greater than or equal to 2.5 standard deviations below young normals (T-score less than -2.5). The possibility of fracture is greater than 4 times that of young normal patients.
